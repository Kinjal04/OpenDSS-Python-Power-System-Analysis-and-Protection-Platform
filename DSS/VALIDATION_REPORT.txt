Design criteria used for the academic study
-------------------------------------------
Normal/peak voltage screening: 0.95 to 1.05 pu at load buses.
27.6-kV feeder rating: 650 A normal / 720 A emergency.
T1: 150 MVA; T2/T3: 40 MVA each; GSU: 30 MVA; UAT/SST: 10 MVA each.
TBESS: 60 MVA; BESS: 50 MW / 200 MWh.
Generator: 20 MW / 25 MVA, +/-15 Mvar capability.
Base connected load: 40.0 MW including 4-MW rail and 3.5-MW auxiliaries.
Source: 230 kV, 1.025 pu Thevenin emf, 5000 MVA 3ph / 3500 MVA SLG.

Expected engineering behavior from independent AC check
--------------------------------------------------------
Normal: min V ~0.972 pu -> PASS.
Peak: min V ~0.953 pu; feeder-B head ~606 A -> PASS normal criteria.
Light: min V ~0.992 pu; system exports a small amount of power -> PASS.
Peak + BESS charge 50 MW: min V ~0.946 pu -> VOLTAGE CONSTRAINT (intentional study finding).
Light + BESS discharge 50 MW: min V ~0.992 pu -> PASS.
Line-230A or Line-230B outage: min V ~0.971 pu -> PASS.
T2 outage + feeder transfer: min V ~0.915 pu, feeder-B head ~753 A -> HIGH-SEVERITY VIOLATION.
T3 outage + feeder transfer: min V ~0.911 pu, feeder-A head ~757 A -> HIGH-SEVERITY VIOLATION.
Generator outage + SST auxiliary supply: min V ~0.973 pu -> PASS.
